# Boosted Commerce

> Boosted Commerce is a Los Angeles based consumer brand platform that acquires and operates
> direct-to-consumer and Amazon FBA businesses in health, wellness, beauty and longevity. It has no
> first-party developer program, but each of its four brand storefronts runs on Shopify and serves a
> live, anonymous agent-commerce surface: a Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) merchant profile, agent
> instructions, and an MCP endpoint with 13 catalog, cart, checkout and order tools.

## Tools

catalog: `search_catalog`, `lookup_catalog`, `get_product`
cart: `create_cart`, `get_cart`, `update_cart`, `cancel_cart`
checkout: `create_checkout`, `get_checkout`, `update_checkout`, `complete_checkout`, `cancel_checkout`
order: `get_order`

`complete_checkout` requires `meta.idempotency-key` and explicit contemporaneous buyer approval of
payment. The MCP endpoint is rate limited per IP; back off on 429.

## Read-only browsing (no authentication)

- `GET /collections/all` and `GET /collections/{handle}/products.json`
- `GET /products/{handle}` and `GET /products/{handle}.json`
- `GET /search?q={query}&type=product`
- `GET /sitemap.xml`
